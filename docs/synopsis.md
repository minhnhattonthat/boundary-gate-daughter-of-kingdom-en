# Synopsis - Boundary Gate: Daughter of Kingdom

For translators and reviewers: who is who, the order of events, and the late reveals. Built from a read of the
extracted event script (`script/EVMES*/`, events in number order) on 2026-11-04. Names per `glossary.md`.
**Don't give a reveal away early**: before chapter 6 nobody knows Risp and Kiral are twins, that Kiral is mostly
artificial, or that the "Ancient Knowledge" is an AI from a starship.

## Setting
The kingdom of **Modalglow**, "the Kingdom of Healing": its doctors and sorcerers can rebuild lost limbs, so the sick
and wounded flock to the capital **Ambrose**. Its magic comes from the **Boundary Gate**, a vast vortex of light, a
door to other worlds. Three temples share power: the **Temple of Knowledge** (Wisebloom; keeps the ancient lore, quiet
scholars), the **Temple of Sorcery** (magic, now the army), the **Temple of Medicine**. A girl who can commune with
the **Ancient Knowledge** is the **Daughter of the Kingdom**, revered by the people; the current one, **Kiral**, lives
in the **Cathedral**. Legend (prologue): the **First Mother** came through endless darkness, built the kingdom in a
night and placed **five Crests** of blessing (Heart, Fang, Iris, Pentagram, and one long lost).

## Chapters (story order)
1. **Arrival in Ambrose** (EVENT00-06). **Finn**, a young mercenary sure of his sword arm, comes to Ambrose to make
   his name (he narrates in first person). A new King has quietly been arming the realm. Finn saves a naive girl,
   **Risp**, lost at night; she came from Wisebloom to see her "younger sister in the castle", and hires him as her
   bodyguard (the Temple of Knowledge will pay). **Rio**, a North District fixer, can get them into the Cathedral if
   they first run an errand to **Caliphos**, where "something has happened". Martial law, rebel hunts.
2. **Caliphos and Rhodedahl** (EVENT07-21). Caliphos has vanished into a crater after a huge blast. Old **Merk**
   tends the wounded. Finn and Risp rescue **Tallman**, who found strange metal fragments. Rio sends the fragments
   to **Grissom** in the snowy mining town **Rhodedahl**, where an old seal has broken in an earthquake and monsters
   roam the mines; old **Hansen** (a lecher with a twinkle) gives Risp the Temple's old seal item and she repairs it.
   Below, Risp finds a small forgotten Gate and reveals she can control Gates. Finn suspects she hides something.
   Interlude (EVENT16): a man (Demius) gloats that his **Homunculi** experiment succeeded and he will rule Modalglow
   once the High Priest and the King are dealt with. EVENT22: in the Cathedral's dark a girl (Kiral) murmurs about
   "moving the ship".
3. **The resistance and Duke Prairial** (EVENT23-31). **Ash**, leader of the resistance against the Temple of
   Sorcery, sends them to the manor of his father **Duke Prairial**, a lord who opposes the Temple of Sorcery (and
   quietly keeps them under watch: Melissa thinks they are Knowledge spies). In the Cathedral, Kiral bends the
   Boundary Gate to summon creatures; **Demius**, Vice High Priest of Sorcery, schemes to rule through her. After
   Finn and Risp save the Duke, his giant, silent retainer **Greystone** joins. Kiral, as Daughter of the Kingdom,
   proclaims martial law and names Demius head of the Temple of Sorcery; alone, she breaks down: "Sister... help me."
4. **Fort Amok** (EVENT32-49). Rio begs them to help Ash rescue his daughter **Sheeda** from the fortress on **Lake
   Elsinore**. On the way Risp tells Finn about Temple life; at Tornam the old boatman **Maillol** ferries them by night
   to the watchtowers. **Melissa**, a sharp-tongued resistance agent ("Thanks, you brute!"), is found locked up; they
   free Sheeda. Monsters attack the Temple of Sorcery's own fortress: Kiral has turned on Demius ("from the start her
   goal was to destroy everything"). Sheeda insists on returning to Ambrose: the imprisoned **new King** is her
   sweetheart. Melissa joins on Ash's orders.
5. **Ambrose under siege** (EVENT50-61). In the Temple of Medicine Risp meets the dying **High Priest of Medicine**,
   who operated on her and her sister. The new King is freed (starving and sheepish). Kiral confronts Risp ("Sister...
   I won't let you stop me"), offers to share the world, and loses herself: "Help me... Sister..."
6. **The twins' secret** (EVENT66-72). The **High Priest of Knowledge** explains: eighteen years ago Risp and Kiral
   were born conjoined, the first Daughters of the Kingdom in fifty years. At seven they were separated; Risp got a
   whole body, Kiral lost almost all of hers and lives on the Temple of Medicine's machines. Both can control the
   Boundary Gate; together they could rule the world. Kiral has twisted the Gate to the brink of runaway. Risp
   confesses she left Wisebloom without permission; Finn decides to go back with her ("Pack up, sheltered little
   miss"). Melissa and Greystone stay too. Risp claims the right to be Daughter: the Ancient Knowledge accepted her in
   the four labyrinths; the last Crest's labyrinth and the Gate lie under the Cathedral.
7. **Beneath the Cathedral / ending** (EVENT75-83, exe ending). Demius, betrayed, dies raving. Kiral, possessed,
   chants of chaos; in the end she thanks her sister ("we'll be together again"). The truth: the ancestors came on a
   **starship**; Caliphos exploded when Kiral tried to fly its buried twin. The Ancient Knowledge is an **artificial
   intelligence** left by the First Mother, keyed to her clones' XX chromosomes: the Daughters are priestesses who
   pass on medical knowledge. Something came through the Gate, a **Mind Parasite**, fed on the AI, planted ambition
   in Demius and took Kiral's mind through her artificial nerves. It is destroyed; the Gate calms. Ending narration:
   Risp's silver hair looks black for an instant (Kiral within her); "Thank you... Finn."

## Voices (detail in docs/style-reference.md)
Finn rough, wry, 俺; Risp polite, earnest, sheltered (私/あたし); Kiral formal and eerie, childlike when herself;
Melissa sassy and bossy (polite ですわ only to Ash); Greystone near-mute ("Mm."); Rio and the North District folk
rough street speech (ねえ); Hansen and Maillol old men (じゃ/わし); Merk an old woman (あたしにゃ); Duke Prairial archaic
and courtly (申す); Ash formal leader; Demius pompous villain, slipping into 俺 when cornered.
